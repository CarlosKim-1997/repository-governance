# Read-only final audit

Inspect authoritative files and observable repository state without rewriting them during the audit. Check for missing or duplicated authority, Canon/reality mismatch, stale Current State, Task authority creep, missing Constraints, inflated OQs or Tasks, unsupported verification claims, and conflicting legacy authority. Check schema/SPEC/checker drift, invalid relation targets, and project-specific leakage into the reusable Core. Record exact evidence and narrow corrections; rerun the audit after material fixes. A checker PASS is structural evidence only.
